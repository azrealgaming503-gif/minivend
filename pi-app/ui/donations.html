Donation tiers

Map donation amounts to chambers. ≥ means "at-least this amount", = means "exactly this amount". Exact matches always win over thresholds.

Chamber labels

Dispense cooldown

Minimum minutes between dispenses, per chamber. A tip that arrives for a chamber still in cooldown is skipped entirely — no drop and no overlay (it's still logged in history). Set 0 to disable a chamber's cooldown. Fractional minutes are fine — 0.5 = 30 sec.

Donation overlay image

A small GIF/image shown in the center of the donation alert, replacing the default sticker. Drag one in or tap to choose. Leave empty to keep the default. Ignored when a StreamElements overlay is assigned to Donations on the Overlays page.

Overlay duration

How long the donation overlay stays on screen
Measured after the dispense finishes (or, for alert-only tips with no drop, the total display time). 1 – 60 seconds.
