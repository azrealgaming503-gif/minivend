Motor controls

Status

checking...
The ESP32 stepper controller is detected automatically when plugged in via USB-serial. If status stays "offline", check the cable and that /dev/minivend-motor exists.

Test dispense

Fires a single dispense pulse on the selected chamber. Useful for clearing a jam, priming a fresh hopper, or double-checking the motor wiring after maintenance.

Dispense motion

Sets which way each chamber turns and how far it spins per dispense, measured in revolutions (partial turns allowed, e.g. 0.5). These apply to both donation drops and the Test dispense buttons above. If one rotation here doesn't equal one real turn of the coil, adjust "Steps per revolution" below until it matches, then re-test.

Chamber 1
Chamber 2
Chamber 2 scale multiplies pulse rate and travel (default 2) so it matches chamber 1 when that driver runs at finer microsteps. Chamber 1 is always 1×.

Manual jog

Press and hold a button to run the motor at low speed in that direction. Release to stop. Useful for aligning a carousel or freeing a half-stuck item without firing a full dispense.

Chamber 1
Chamber 2
Steps per second at 1/16 microstep. ~19200 matches the old full-step jog feel; lower only if you need a slow crawl. The motor stops the instant you lift your finger.

Emergency stop

Cuts power to both stepper drivers immediately. Use if you see a jam developing or hear an unusual noise.

If shafts stay locked when idle: Force cool idle, then check the log for M1_TOFF=0 and EN_GPIO. Still locked? Try Flip EN polarity. Measure the driver EN pad — with active-low idle it should be ~3.3 V.
